# Halloween dos Freitas

This glossary defines the event, guest, contest, voting, and photo concepts
used by the annual party product.

## Event and participation

**Edition**:
One annual instance of the Halloween dos Freitas party, identified by its
stable event ID and labeled by its year and slug.
_Avoid_: Event year, party (when referring to its data identity)

**RSVP**:
A guest's attendance registration for one edition. An RSVP establishes guest
eligibility and is separate from entering the costume contest.
_Avoid_: Contest registration, contestant

**Contestant entry**:
A person's or group's entry in one edition's costume contest, with its own
stable identity, owner, category, readiness, and contest photo.
_Avoid_: RSVP, participant (when referring specifically to a ballot candidate)

**Enrollment cutoff**:
The permanent point for one edition when first opening voting ends new RSVPs
and contestant additions or deletions.
_Avoid_: Registration close (without naming which registration)

## Voting and photos

**Voting round**:
One ballot period for an edition and its categories. An edition can have the
primary round and one runoff round.
_Avoid_: Voting session

**Party photo**:
A guest-uploaded image of the party, separate from a contestant entry's
required contest photo.
_Avoid_: Contest photo, participant photo

**Party album**:
The event-scoped collection of party photos controlled by one publication state
for that edition.
_Avoid_: Contestant gallery
